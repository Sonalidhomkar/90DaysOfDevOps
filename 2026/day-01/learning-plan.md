# 90 Days DevOps Learning Plan

## About Me

I am a BBA(CA) graduate and a fresher who is currently building my career in DevOps and Cloud Engineering. I have basic knowledge of AWS, Linux, Git & GitHub, networking, and shell scripting.

## My Understanding of DevOps & Cloud

DevOps is a combination of development and operations practices that helps teams build, deploy, and manage applications efficiently through automation, collaboration, and continuous improvement.

Cloud Engineering involves using cloud platforms such as AWS to create, manage, secure, and scale infrastructure and applications.

## Why I Am Learning DevOps & Cloud

I want to build a career as a DevOps & Cloud Engineer. I want to gain practical experience by working with real tools, projects, automation, and cloud infrastructure.

## My 3 Goals for the Next 90 Days

1. Build strong practical knowledge of Linux, Git, Docker, AWS, and CI/CD.
2. Deploy and automate a real-world application using DevOps tools and practices.
3. Become confident and job-ready for a fresher DevOps/Cloud Engineer role.

## 3 Core Skills I Want to Build

- Linux & Troubleshooting
- AWS & Cloud Infrastructure
- Docker & CI/CD

## My Weekly Time Commitment

- Monday–Friday: 2 hours per day
- Saturday–Sunday: 3–4 hours per day
- Total: Approximately 16–18 hours per week

## My Consistency Plan

I will learn and practice every day instead of only studying theory. I will maintain my GitHub repository, document my learning, build hands-on projects, revise previous topics, and share my progress on LinkedIn.

If I miss a day, I will continue from where I stopped instead of giving up.

## My 90-Day Commitment

My goal is not to learn every DevOps tool. My goal is to understand the fundamentals, practice consistently, build real projects, and become confident enough to start my DevOps career.
